# Post-hoc loss attribution: odds cohorts

After seeing that both Elo challengers lose on the 2025 retrospective tennis
evaluation, break their hypothetical results down by selected quoted odds:
`1–<2`, `2–<4`, and `4+`. The same 2,256-event evaluation denominator, decisions,
stake and assumed fee are retained. Also distinguish whether the chosen side was
the quoted favorite (ties included) or underdog. These are descriptive, post-hoc
cohorts; do not use them to select a profitable threshold on the same dataset or
interpret them as independently tested forecasts. Keep small or losing cohorts.

The quotes are unverified best closing prices, not accepted fills; the source is
tennis rather than table tennis. No output grants product promotion or real-money
authority. The original aggregate report is frozen at
`79eed0ac1c78c4d6362e75d657967558585620d2`.
