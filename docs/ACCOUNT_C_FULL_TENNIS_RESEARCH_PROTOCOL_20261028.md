# Full public tennis file: chronological exploratory economics

Source: minmaxxxer, ATP/WTA Closing Odds 2025, CC BY 4.0,
https://huggingface.co/datasets/minmaxxxer/atp-wta-closing-odds-2025.
The already publicly released source has roughly 5,791 rows. Earlier research
inspected its first 100 rows; the remainder has not been analyzed in this work.
This file has been published and used elsewhere and is not represented as an
independently registered prospective holdout. Tennis is not table tennis.

Use publisher-reported match outcomes and best closing prices. Train/update Elo
from 2024-12-27 through 2025-06-30; evaluate all eligible events from 2025-07-01
through 2025-12-31. Both players must have one earlier completed result for the
guarded challenger. Compare no bet, market favorite, unguarded Elo and guarded
Elo with the existing fixed K16 and 0.05 probability-edge threshold. All models
use the same evaluation population, one hypothetical stake unit and assumed 2%
fee on positive winnings. Update only after a UTC day completes.

The source selects already recorded games, may omit games and events, and has
not been independently authenticated. Best closing odds can be from different
books, at unknown timestamps and may not have been simultaneously executable.
No actual fill, sports settlement, costs, limits or bankroll sequence is proven.
Report all exclusions, wins and losses, gross/net return, turnover, ROI and
drawdown in match-start order, together with full input and evaluator hashes.
No strategy promotion, forward evidence, real-money operation or real profit
claim is authorized by this retrospective diagnostic. Retain unfavorable results.
