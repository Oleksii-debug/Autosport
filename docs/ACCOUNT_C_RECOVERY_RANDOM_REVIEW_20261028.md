# Synthetic random-walk recovery stress test

The same 120-decision random-walk snapshots were supplied to the existing rule
and both research-only recovery schedules for each of the eight fixed seeds in
`ACCOUNT_C_RECOVERY_RANDOM_PROTOCOL_20261028.md`.

All eight cases tied exactly across all three arms for simulated net reward,
drawdown, ticket count and WAIT count. Every arm placed 120 tickets and had
zero WAIT decisions; neither recovery schedule was exercised. This is therefore
**inconclusive about recovery**, not eight wins for a proposed strategy.

The random-walk generator and its artificial quote-movement payout gave positive
synthetic net rewards in all eight cases. That is a property of this particular
simulation setup, not a verified sports edge or a representative profit forecast.
Do not use these figures to justify promotion, larger stakes, or claims about real
money. Continue with genuinely different regimes and independently observed
outcomes, prices and costs before any economic conclusion.

The JSON retains all seeds, source/snapshot hashes and the false promotion,
forward-evidence, real-money and profitability flags. Eight distinct cases and
the source hash were independently checked after the run.
