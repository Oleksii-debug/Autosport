# Synthetic regime stress: results

Eight fixed seeded cases compared the existing rule and two research-only
recovery schedules on identical price snapshots. Data, payoff and positive
rewards are synthetic; these are not match outcomes or evidence of real profit.

In all four continuing-loss cases both proposed schedules lost more than the
current abstention rule. Additional synthetic loss ranged from 88 to 174 UAH
for the 40-step schedule and 44 to 131 UAH for the 37-step schedule. Both also
incurred larger drawdown in every continuing-loss case.

In four recovery cases, the 40-step schedule had higher synthetic net reward
in all four. The 37-step schedule had higher reward in three and lower reward
in one: seed 211 ended at -1167 versus -1123 UAH for the current rule. This
one failure demonstrates that even a favorable later segment does not ensure
an exploratory action observes the recovery in time. Both can reopen ordinary
exploitation once a mean becomes positive; the total number of tickets is not
limited to the scheduled probes.

No proposed policy is promoted. The small, constructed sample, retrospective
design and simulated price-direction payout preclude statistical or real-world
economic conclusions. All eight cases and source/snapshot hashes are retained in
the JSON, including the failure; count, uniqueness and source hash were checked.
