# Cold-start abstention: post-hoc exploratory result

This is the same already inspected 100-row CC BY 4.0 preview and 65 eligible
evaluation matches described in `ACCOUNT_C_VISIBLE_TENNIS_PREVIEW_REVIEW_20261028.md`.
The candidate was chosen after seeing the first results; no independent holdout,
verified fills, actual bookmaker costs or product promotion is implied.

| Hypothetical policy | Bets | Wins / losses | Net units | ROI |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Elo without history guard | 58 | 9 / 49 | -27.9202 | -48.14% |
| Elo requiring both prior players | 8 | 1 / 7 | -4.55 | -56.875% |

One-unit stakes and an assumed 2% fee on winning profits were used. The guard
reduced absolute loss and exposure, but ROI per unit staked became worse. With
only eight bets and postselected data this does not establish improvement or
profitability. Both options remain rejected as product promotions. Tennis
findings do not establish a table-tennis edge.

Forty-seven evaluated events had no previously observed match for either player,
ten had one known player, and eight had both; within-day results are unavailable
to the decision. Eight focused tests passed, including a history-guard denominator
test and same-day causality. Exact output, adapted CSV and source attribution are
retained; historical prices are not independently verified accepted fills.
