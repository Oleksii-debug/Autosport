# Retrospective monthly stability diagnostic

After seeing the total losses of the market-anchored research policy, break down
the already consumed July–December 2025 tennis evaluation by calendar month.
The existing decisions, early-data learning, one-unit stake and assumed fee are
unchanged. Retain every month, including negative and zero-bet periods. Monthly
profit is independently reconciled against the overall aggregate.

This is a post-hoc descriptive analysis of reported best closing prices and match
outcomes, not live execution, a fresh holdout or grounds for model promotion.
Monthly period marks do not establish independence or risk-adjusted profitability.
