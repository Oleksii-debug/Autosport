# Post-hoc probability-quality diagnostic

The 2025 tennis evaluation and outcome/odds have already been examined.
On the same 2,256 eligible later matches, compare the mean Brier score and log
loss of three predicted probabilities: Elo, normalized reciprocal of the two
reported best closing odds, and the fixed 75% market / 25% Elo blend. Scores
include all eligible events, not just bets; lower is better. Same-day outcomes
remain withheld. Scores are diagnostic of this dataset only and cannot prove
the source timestamps, accepted fills, real profits or table-tennis skill.

This is post-hoc exploratory analysis, not fresh holdout or promotion evidence.
The two best prices may come from different books at different times, so even
the so-called market proxy is not independently verified consensus. No runtime
policy, money, risk cap or learning memory is changed.
