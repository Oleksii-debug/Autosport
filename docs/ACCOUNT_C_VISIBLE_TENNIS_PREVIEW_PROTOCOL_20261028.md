# Public tennis preview: retrospective pilot, not a holdout

Source: minmaxxxer, `atp-wta-closing-odds-2025`, CC BY 4.0,
https://huggingface.co/datasets/minmaxxxer/atp-wta-closing-odds-2025.
The public dataset viewer exposes a small, ordered preview of reported outcomes,
player IDs and best closing quotes. Its sample is already visible and has been
partly inspected before this protocol: it is a **postselected exploratory pilot**,
not an untouched or representative holdout. Preserve the exact preview content
hash, retrieval date and every exclusion; no raw personal data is required in the
final aggregate report.

Use visible matches before 2024-12-30 as warmup, 2024-12-30 through 2024-12-31
as evaluation; batch updates only after each UTC match day. Compare no bet,
market favorite and a fixed Elo K16/edge 0.05 challenger, hypothetical 1-unit
stake and hypothetical 2% winning-profit fee. For this pilot use the reported
`best_close_odds1/2`, not fair odds: no evidence establishes that the best
prices from different bookmakers were simultaneously or actually executable.

Outcome/source rights, result publication timestamps and settlement rules are
publisher assertions not independently verified. Matches with absent/invalid
scores or prices are excluded and counted. No raw or aggregate result enters
product memory, risk authority or forward population. Tennis is not table tennis.
No real-money, live-execution, promotion, significant edge or profit claim is made.
