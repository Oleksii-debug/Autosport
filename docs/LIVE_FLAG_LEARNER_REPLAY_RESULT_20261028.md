# Recorded live-flag learner replay: negative result

The previously declared chronological diagnostic on the source-time sensitivity
cohort contained 69 settled signals. All signals, scored at source-recorded odds,
would have returned +7.000 units after 1% costs. The existing unseeded learner chose
16 of the 69 and WAIT on 53; its selections produced -0.150 units, ROI -0.94%,
with 6 wins and 10 losses and 7.100 units maximum kickoff-ordered drawdown. The
remaining 15 outcomes were not yet eligible for the *last decision*, although
all 69 were eventually settled in the retrospective report.

The learner therefore did not preserve the full cohort's historical gain on this
sample. This does not establish that either strategy has a real edge: the source
subset was previously examined, the alternative source-time rule was selected
post-hoc, the sample is small and its day-block confidence ranges include large
losses and gains. Result availability was assumed at kickoff +48 hours, and
source prices, timing, actual liquidity and order acceptance are unverified.
Kickoff-ordered drawdown is descriptive rather than a verified cash ledger.

This is separate from the new seeded challenger: the replay starts unseeded,
whereas the challenger was initialized with the 69 recorded outcomes at its
actual later start time. The replay's negative result is retained and must inform
interpretation of any future apparent profit. Twelve focused tests passed,
including the 30-observation gate, delayed labels, WAIT learning and unchanged
current choice when its own future outcome changes. Attribution: TipsAudit
